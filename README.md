# Franklin Alegu

**Founder & Creative Director | Brand, Visual Identity, Website & Product Designer | Creative Technologist**

Franklin Alegu is a multidisciplinary designer, creative technologist and software engineering professional focused on helping businesses, organizations and digital products transform ideas into meaningful brands, compelling visual identities and purposeful digital experiences.

As the Founder and Creative Director of **MRJAMESBRAND LTD**, Franklin leads a creative practice that brings together brand strategy, visual identity, website design, product design, digital marketing, technology and emerging AI tools. His approach goes beyond aesthetics. He combines creative thinking with business and technology to develop brands and digital experiences that are strategic, functional, memorable and built for growth.

His professional journey spans **brand and visual communication, product design, website development, software engineering, digital marketing, 3D product modelling and AI-enabled creative workflows**. This multidisciplinary foundation allows him to understand projects from both creative and technical perspectives, bridging the gap between how a product looks, how it works and how it connects with its audience.

Franklin has trained and developed expertise across several areas of technology and design. His professional development includes a **12-month Full Stack Software Engineering programme with ALX**, with a front-end specialization completed in 2023, alongside Product Design training through **Stutern and DevCareer/UK-Nigeria Tech Hub**. He has also developed skills in Product Modelling through **Orange VFX**, completed the **AWS AI Practitioner Challenge through Udacity**, and pursued AI education through **Anthropic's AI Fluency for Educators and Claude 101**.

His digital marketing experience further strengthens his ability to connect design with measurable business outcomes. His training and practical exposure include **SEO, paid social advertising, Google Search, content marketing, email automation, lead generation, retargeting, community management, analytics, A/B testing and performance reporting**.

Across his work, Franklin is particularly interested in the relationship between **people, brands, products and technology**. He believes effective design should not simply make something look better; it should make ideas clearer, experiences easier and businesses more credible.

His work therefore moves across the full creative and digital spectrum: from **strategy and brand identity to websites, digital products, marketing experiences and technology-enabled systems**.

Through MRJAMESBRAND LTD, Franklin continues to build a body of work centered on one fundamental idea:

> **Design should create clarity, communicate value and move businesses forward.**
